FAQ

Questions

Is AIHub DJ really free?

Yes. Free to download and use, no account and no subscription.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only for YouTube and cover art. Your own music files play offline, and the AI DJ can run fully on your computer with Ollama.

What do I need for the AI DJ?

Install Ollama and a small model (for example ollama pull llama3.2:3b). AIHub DJ finds it automatically. Nothing you ask leaves your machine.

Which music files can I play?

MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, OGG and AAC, plus music videos (MP4, MKV, MOV), alongside anything you search on YouTube.

Is this the same DJ as in AIHub Browser?

Yes. It is the DJ console from AIHub Browser as its own app, for when you just want to DJ.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows SmartScreen warns about new apps that are not code-signed yet. Click “More info” and “Run anyway” to install.
